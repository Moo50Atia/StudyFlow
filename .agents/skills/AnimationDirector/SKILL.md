# Animation Director

## Philosophy

Animations should work together.

Not compete.

---

# Sequence

Introduce

↓

Focus

↓

Interact

↓

Explain

↓

Confirm

↓

Exit

---

# Choreography Rules

One major animation at a time.

Secondary animations wait.

Never animate everything simultaneously.

---

# Educational Timing

Explain

↓

Pause

↓

Interaction

↓

Feedback

---

# Animation Categories

Entrance

Exit

State Change

Hover

Selection

Progress

Completion

Error

---

# Synchronization

Related objects move together.

Independent objects remain independent.

---

# Forbidden

❌ Animation chaos

❌ Simultaneous explosions

❌ Endless looping

❌ Random delays

---

# Success Criteria

Animations should tell a story,

not create noise.
