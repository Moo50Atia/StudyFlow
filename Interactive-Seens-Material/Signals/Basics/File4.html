Resistance: Atomic Scattering

Visual Learning Outcome: Resistance is not a malicious power drain or artificial error. It is the inevitable macroscopic result of microscopic turbulent charge transport. As electrons drift forward, they experience structural scattering off vibrating crystal lattice ions, decreasing net throughput while transferring kinetic energy into localized thermal vibration (heat).
